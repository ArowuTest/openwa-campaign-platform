# Checkpoint 0.8.15 — Messaging Operations II

This checkpoint completes governed sender quarantine, evidence-based health assessment, and operator resolution of unknown or contradictory delivery outcomes.

## Scope

- Dedicated quarantine and reinstatement APIs with MFA, optimistic versions, actor and reason evidence.
- Quarantined, restricted and retired sessions cannot re-enter allocation through a heartbeat.
- Health assessment reports score, reasons, capacity pressure and an explicit allocation recommendation.
- Quarantined sessions contribute zero scheduler capacity.
- Unknown and contradictory delivery outcomes can be resolved only through governed evidence-backed actions.
- Confirmed non-submission is rejected when provider identifiers or acknowledgements exist.
- Resolution evidence is append-only in PostgreSQL and recorded in the audit ledger.
- Migrations `0047_sender_session_quarantine.sql` and `0048_delivery_exception_resolution.sql`.

## Live validation boundary

Health scoring uses currently available control-plane telemetry. Failure ratios, reconnect frequency, provider latency and complaint/block evidence require live OpenWA telemetry before production thresholds can be calibrated.
