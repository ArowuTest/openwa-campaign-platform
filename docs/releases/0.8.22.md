# 0.8.22 — Reporting and financial reconciliation

This checkpoint expands canonical reporting without relying on transient queue state.

## Campaign financial reconciliation

A new campaign reconciliation snapshot ties together:

- commercial approval and payment evidence;
- approved recipient volume;
- actual recipient obligations;
- provider-accepted, sent, delivered, read, failed and unknown outcomes;
- approved amount and currency;
- recipient-volume variance;
- explicit balanced, under-allocated, over-allocated, payment-missing and commercial-evidence-missing states.

The report does not invent a billable-unit policy. It presents authoritative commercial and delivery evidence so Finance and Operations can reconcile the campaign under the organisation's approved charging rules.

## Organisation performance reporting

A new organisation-level report aggregates:

- campaign lifecycle totals;
- authorised, suppressed and opted-out recipients;
- queued, submitted, sent, delivered, read, failed and unknown outcomes;
- approved recipient volume and approved amount grouped by currency;
- unresolved-outcome and missing-commercial-evidence warnings.

## Campaign report enrichment

The organisation-facing campaign report includes commercial approval, payment and multi-pool route evidence, including OpenWA engine, reserved capacity and canonical outcomes by sender pool.

## APIs

- `GET /api/v1/campaigns/{id}/financial-reconciliation`
- `GET /api/v1/organisations/{id}/performance-report`

The OpenAPI contract covers all 166 implemented `/api/v1` method/path pairs.

## Validation boundary

The queries and service logic are covered by automated tests and compilation. Live PostgreSQL execution, production-volume query plans and financial acceptance testing remain external release gates.
