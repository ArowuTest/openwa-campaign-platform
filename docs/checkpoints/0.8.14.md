# Checkpoint 0.8.14 — Messaging Operations I

This checkpoint adds governed durable-job operations and queue back-pressure to the messaging core.

Implemented:

- Operational job search by type and status.
- Queue summary counters, oldest-pending evidence and lease-health counts.
- MFA-protected dead-letter replay with mandatory reason.
- MFA-protected cancellation of pending work with mandatory reason.
- Append-only administrative evidence for job replay and cancellation.
- PostgreSQL indexes for pending and dead-letter operational review.
- Configurable dispatch-queue back-pressure at the transactional-outbox publication boundary.
- Retry-aware back-pressure delay without dropping the authoritative outbox obligation.
- OpenAPI coverage for the new operations routes.

The control plane still treats PostgreSQL as authoritative. Back-pressure delays publication; it does not delete or silently skip campaign obligations.
